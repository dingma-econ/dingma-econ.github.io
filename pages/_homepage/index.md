## About Me

Welcome! I am an applied microeconomist and an Assistant Professor at the College of Economics and Management, [Nanjing Agricultural University](https://www.njau.edu.cn/). My research lies at the intersection of environmental economics and health economics. I use applied microeconomic methods to study how climate change, water pollution, and environmental policy shape firm behavior, population health, and social welfare.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the [National School of Development](https://nsd.pku.edu.cn/), [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/), in June 2026, under the supervision of [Prof. Min Wang](https://en.nsd.pku.edu.cn/faculty/fulltime/w/240007.htm). During 2024–2025, I was a Postgraduate Fellow at the [Charles H. Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management](https://dyson.cornell.edu/), Cornell University, working with [Prof. Jinhua Zhao](https://dyson.cornell.edu/faculty-research/faculty/jz638/). Before that, I obtained a B.S. in Environmental Science and a B.A. in Economics from Peking University in 2021.

## Research Interests

- **Environmental Economics:** environmental policy; climate change; water pollution
- **Health Economics:** environmental health; nutrition
