About Me

Welcome! I am an Assistant Professor at the College of Economics and Management, Nanjing Agricultural University. My research lies at the intersection of environmental economics and health economics. I use applied microeconomic methods to study how climate change, water pollution, and environmental policy shape firm behavior, population health, and social welfare.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the National School of Development, Peking University, in June 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Min Wang. During 2024–2025, I was a Postgraduate Fellow at the Charles H. Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management, Cornell University, working with Prof. Jinhua Zhao. Before that, I obtained a B.S. in Environmental Science and a B.A. in Economics from Peking University in 2021.

Research Interests

  • Environmental Economics: environmental policy; climate change; water pollution
  • Health Economics: environmental health; nutrition
